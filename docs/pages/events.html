Prototype 5 of 5 · Events

What we're proposing

Event listings that show, before anyone clicks, whether places are left, whether it's online or in person, who it's for and what it costs. The details open in a panel with booking, a waiting list when it's full, and an add-to-calendar file. Events move to their own section, out of /membership2/.

Why

ABPI Code training is promoted as "now available to book" on the events page, while both remaining dates are marked FULL further down its own page. The Code page lists the same course under 10 August; the events page says 11 May to 6 November. Prices aren't shown anywhere, and on the homepage the event tiles print their full descriptions, so a row of four grows to over a thousand pixels tall.

Event titles and dates are real. Status, places and prices are sample data.

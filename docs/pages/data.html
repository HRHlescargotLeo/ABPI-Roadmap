Prototype 3 of 5 · Industry in numbers

What we're proposing

Replace the Facts and figures page with a page built for the people who quote the ABPI's numbers. Figures are grouped by topic, each carries its source and date, and each can be copied with the source attached. Every topic has a chart with its data available to download, and two of the ABPI's strongest arguments, clinical trials and access to medicines, get views of their own.

Why

The current page has the right figures, but two captions stop mid-word ("a significantly lower pro"), and some figures cite references [9] and [12] when the list ends at [7]. The homepage repeats three of the figures with reference numbers that point nowhere on that page. Jobs are given as 125,702 on this page and "over 73,000" on the Careers page, which may well be two different measures, but nothing says so. A journalist or a civil servant who notices any of this is less likely to trust the rest.

Headline figures and sources are as published on abpi.org.uk. Chart values other than the published figures, the set-up time tracker and the access counts are sample data. Items in [brackets] need the ABPI to confirm.

Clinical trials: are set-up times coming down?

The government has committed to cutting the time it takes to set up a commercial clinical trial in England from 250 days to 150. This tracker shows the median set-up time each quarter against that target.

Read more in our report UK industry clinical trials: Translating actions into impact and on the clinical research topic page.

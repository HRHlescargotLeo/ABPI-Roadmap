Prototype 1 of 5 · Membership enquiry

What we're proposing

An enquiry in three short steps that goes straight to Membership Services, with the category filled in if the visitor came from the checker. The formal application, with a signature, comes later and only for companies that go ahead.

Why

The current route asks for a full, signed application before anyone at the ABPI has spoken to the company. Splitting enquiry from application means the team hears from more companies, earlier, and the questions asked at this stage are only the ones needed to have a useful first conversation.

Nothing is sent from this prototype. The fields are a starting point for the membership team to adjust.

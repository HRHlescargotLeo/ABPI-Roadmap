Prototype 5 of 5 · Media centre

What we're proposing

A media centre that separates press releases, statements and blogs, with the press office and spokespeople on the same page and a sign-up for embargoed releases.

Why

The current Media Centre filters by two types, Blogs and News, so a journalist looking for the ABPI's response to a minister resigning finds it among research announcements. The introduction also invites people to "call the Press Office at PressOffice@abpi.org.uk", which is harder than it sounds. Small fixes, but this is the page journalists land on first.

Titles and dates are real. Splitting "News" into press releases and statements is our reading of each item and would need checking.

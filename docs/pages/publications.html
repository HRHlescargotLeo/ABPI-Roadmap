Prototype 4 of 5 · Publications

What we're proposing

A search box at the top of the publications library, twelve themes that match the "Our focus" menu in place of around 38, a type filter that includes consultation responses, and a date, type, format and written summary on every result.

Why

The library holds 131 publications and the only way to search it is the site-wide search in the header. The theme filter has grown one tag at a time: many themes hold a single item, and some are near-duplicates ("ATMP" and "Advanced Therapy Medicinal Products (ATMPs)"; "Clinical research" and "Clinical trials", seven items each). Several results show breadcrumb text in place of a summary, and none shows a date. The content is good. It's the library around it that makes it hard to find.

Titles are real. Some dates, all reading times and the two consultation responses are sample data, and are marked as such.

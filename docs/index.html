Five ideas for abpi.org.uk

Clickable prototypes of five improvements to the ABPI website, aimed at the people who use it most: member companies, companies thinking of joining, policymakers and journalists. Everything that should work does, so you can try each one as a visitor would.

Version 2 30 September 2026 Designed prototypes Sample data where marked

The prototypes

  1. Join the ABPI
    1

    Join the ABPI online

    The three membership categories side by side, a short checker that says which one fits, how fees are worked out, and an online enquiry in three steps in place of the signed, posted application form.

    Try: answer the three questions in the checker, then follow it through to the enquiry.

    Open prototype
  2. Members
    2

    A member directory and a front door for members

    A searchable directory with therapy areas and UK sites, plus a member area with sign-in, briefings, groups members can join, and locked previews that show non-members what they're missing.

    Try: find the members working in rare diseases, then open the member area and sign in with any details.

    Open prototype
  3. Industry in numbers
    3

    The industry in numbers

    Every headline figure grouped by topic, each with its source and date and a one-click "copy with source". Charts with downloadable data, a clinical trials tracker, and the access-to-medicines graphic made filterable.

    Try: open Clinical trials and copy a figure, then filter access to medicines to oncology in 2025.

    Open prototype
  4. Publications
    4

    Find any publication or position

    Search inside the publications library, twelve themes instead of around 38, and a date, type and proper summary on every result. Plus a topic hub template, shown with clinical research.

    Try: search for "clinical trials", then narrow to consultation responses.

    Open prototype
  5. Stay informed
    5

    Stay informed

    Email updates by topic, a media centre that separates press releases, statements and blogs, and event listings that say plainly whether places are left.

    Try: sign up for two topics, then open ABPI Code training in events and join the waiting list.

    Open prototype

How to review

  • This version applies the ABPI's own look: the aubergine, plum and magenta palette, Poppins, square corners and the split hero with a photograph. Photography is the ABPI's own, loaded from abpi.org.uk; where it can't load, a brand-colour illustration shows instead.
  • Everything interactive works: the checker, forms and their error messages, search and filters, charts, sign-in, booking panels. Links to pages outside these five go nowhere by design.
  • Use the bar at the top to move between prototypes. Switch Notes on (top right) to see what each prototype proposes and why, plus notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for the ABPI (red). Notes start switched off.
  • Member company names, statistics, publication titles and event titles are real, taken from abpi.org.uk on 30 September 2026. Therapy areas, sites, prices, places and anything marked "sample" are illustrative.
  • Works on a phone: open this page on your mobile to check the layouts.

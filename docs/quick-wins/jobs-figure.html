Quick win 9 of 34 · Facts and figures

What we're proposing

Use the same jobs figure on the Facts page and the Careers page, or say why they differ.

Why

The Facts and figures page says the industry supports 125,702 jobs. The Careers page says it "directly employs over 73,000 people". They may well be two different measures, but nothing on either page says so, and anyone comparing them will assume one is wrong.

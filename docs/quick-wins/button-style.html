Quick win 32 of 34 · Look, feel and accessibility

What we're proposing

One primary and one secondary button style, in sentence case, in place of three styles and two magentas.

Why

The homepage alone has at least three button styles: two uppercase versions at different sizes and weights, and a sentence-case one, using two very slightly different magentas. Settling on one primary and one secondary style is a stylesheet change, and it makes every call to action look like it belongs to the same site.

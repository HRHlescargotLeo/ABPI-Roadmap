Quick win 14 of 34 · News, media and email

What we're proposing

A short sign-up (email and one tick box) in the footer of every page, linking to a fuller preference page.

Why

We couldn't find a newsletter or email alert sign-up anywhere on the site. The ABPI publishes something most weeks, and the people who would most like to hear about it have to remember to come and look. A footer sign-up connected to the email platform the ABPI already uses is the smallest version of this. Try submitting it.

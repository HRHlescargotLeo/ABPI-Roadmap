Quick win 30 of 34 · Accuracy and housekeeping

What we're proposing

List what each external script is for, and remove the ones nobody uses.

Why

The homepage calls twelve third-party addresses, including two site-quality tools that seem to do similar jobs (Siteimprove and Silktide), Ahrefs, Google Analytics, a sharing widget, a feedback widget and the search service. Some of these will be essential. Fewer scripts mean faster pages and a simpler cookie banner, and the review itself is a short job.

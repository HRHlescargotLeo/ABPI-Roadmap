Quick win 6 of 34 · Joining the ABPI

What we're proposing

Point "Become an ABPI member" at the joining journey, rather than at the same page as "ABPI Membership".

Why

In the Membership menu, "ABPI Membership" and "Become an ABPI member" both go to /membership/. The second one looks like the way in, so it should go somewhere different: the checker or the enquiry. It's a change to one menu item.

Quick win 20 of 34 · Events

What we're proposing

A status label on every event (booking open, few places, full, register interest), driven by one field.

Why

ABPI Code training is listed as "now available to book" while both remaining dates are marked FULL further down its own page, and the Code page lists it under a third date. A single status field, shown on the listing, the event page and the homepage, means they can't disagree.

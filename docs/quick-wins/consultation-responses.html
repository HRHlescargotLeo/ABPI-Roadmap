Quick win 13 of 34 · Publications

What we're proposing

Add "Consultation response" as a publication type, with the consulting body and closing date on each.

Why

The ABPI responds to a steady stream of consultations, but the publication types are Report, Guidelines, Industry information, Key messages, Posters, Presentation, Partnerships and Code of Practice. Responses end up filed as one of those, if they are published at all. A type of its own makes them easy to find for the officials who asked for them.

Quick win 21 of 34 · Events

What we're proposing

Shorter descriptions on event cards, with format, audience and price shown as labels.

Why

On the homepage the event tiles print their full descriptions, so one long description stretches the whole row to over a thousand pixels tall. Cards say "Physical Public & Paid" but not the price. Limiting descriptions to three lines and showing the essentials as labels is a template change.

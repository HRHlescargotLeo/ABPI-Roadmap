Quick win 10 of 34 · Facts and figures

What we're proposing

Show where each figure comes from wherever it appears, starting with the homepage.

Why

The homepage shows three headline statistics with reference numbers [1], [2] and [3], but the references themselves are on a different page. A source line under each figure, drawn from the same record as the Facts page, means anyone quoting the figure from the homepage can cite it properly.

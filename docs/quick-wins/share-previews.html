Quick win 15 of 34 · News, media and email

What we're proposing

Fill in the title, description and image that social sites and messaging apps use when a page is shared.

Why

The Media Centre's share tags (og:title, og:description, og:image) are empty, and the homepage has no share image, so a link posted on LinkedIn or sent in Teams shows up as a bare address. Defaults for each page type, with overrides for reports and news, fix it everywhere at once.

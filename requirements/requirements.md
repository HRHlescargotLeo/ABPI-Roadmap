# ABPI — prototype requirements (V1)

Source: ClerksWell phase 1 review of abpi.org.uk and five comparator associations (30 Sep 2026).
Idea numbers (#nn) refer to the Opportunities page. These are ClerksWell proposals, not
client requirements; the ABPI has not yet reviewed them.

## Global
- R01 A prototype navigator replaces the ABPI header; a previous/next pager replaces the footer.
- R02 Every template works at 320px, 768px and 1024px+.
- R03 All interactive components are keyboard operable and dismissible with Escape (WCAG 2.2 AA target) (#42, #54).
- R04 Buttons use one primary and one secondary style, sentence case (#50).
- R05 Every statistic shown carries its source and date (#15).

## Prototype 1 — Join the ABPI (ideas #1–#7)
- R10 A membership overview with three categories side by side and one clear call to action (#7).
- R11 A "Which membership fits us?" checker: up to three questions, a recommended category, a link to the enquiry with the category pre-filled (#2).
- R12 A plain explanation of how fees are calculated for each category (#3).
- R13 Benefits grouped into four blocks with a figure and a member quote each (placeholders until the ABPI supplies them) (#4).
- R14 A named membership contact on the page (#5).
- R15 A route for smaller and emerging companies (#6).
- R16 An online enquiry in three steps (organisation, your work in the UK, contact) with validation, replacing the posted form (#1).
- R17 A confirmation that says what happens next and when.

## Prototype 2 — Members (ideas #8–#11)
- R20 A member directory with keyword search, category filter, therapy area filter, list and grid views (#8).
- R21 Each member shows logo, name, category, therapy areas, UK sites and a link to the company (#8).
- R22 A visible "Member area" entry point (#9).
- R23 A member area landing page: sign in, latest briefings, groups, member events (#9).
- R24 Locked teasers that show a non-member what the briefing is and how to get it (#10).
- R25 A list of groups and committees with remit, chair and how to apply (#11).

## Prototype 3 — Industry in numbers (ideas #13–#18)
- R30 Figures grouped by topic: economy, research and development, clinical trials, access to medicines, people (#16).
- R31 Each figure shows its source, date and a "Copy with source" action (#13, #15).
- R32 A chart per topic with a plain-English summary under it and a data download (#16).
- R33 A clinical trials tracker: trials started by year and set-up times against the government target (#17).
- R34 An access-to-medicines view filterable by year and therapy area (#18).
- R35 Figures that differ between pages are reconciled or explained (#14).

## Prototype 4 — Publications and topics (ideas #19–#24)
- R40 Keyword search inside the publications library, with the query kept in the URL (#19).
- R41 Filters for theme (12–15 themes), type (including consultation responses) and year, with active filter chips (#20, #23).
- R42 Every result shows date, type, format and a written summary (#21).
- R43 Sort by newest or relevance; load more (#19).
- R44 A topic hub template: position in brief, key figures, latest publications, news, events, contacts (#22).
- R45 Report pages offer key findings, a suggested citation and chart downloads (#24).

## Prototype 5 — Stay informed (ideas #25, #26, #32–#34)
- R50 An email preference centre: topics, frequency, press releases, with validation and confirmation (#25).
- R51 A media centre separating press releases, statements and blogs, with filters (#26).
- R52 Press office contacts and spokespeople (#26).
- R53 Event listings with status (booking open, few places, full, waiting list), format, audience and price (#32, #33).
- R54 Event descriptions clamped; details and booking in a panel with add-to-calendar (#33, #34).
- R55 Events live at /events/, not under membership (#35).

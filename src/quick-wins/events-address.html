What we're proposing

Move events from /membership2/events/ to /events/, with redirects from the old addresses.

Why

Events live at /membership2/events/ and the page title reads "Events | Membership", although many of the events are open to everyone. A top-level /events/ section with redirects is a small piece of configuration, and it makes event links read properly when they are shared.

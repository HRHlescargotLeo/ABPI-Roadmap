Quick win 3 of 34 · Joining the ABPI

What we're proposing

Four benefit blocks, each with a figure and a member quote, in place of long paragraphs.

Why

The benefits of membership are described in long paragraphs ("We work for our members in two ways…") with no numbers and no member voices. Four short blocks, each with one figure the ABPI can stand behind and a line from a member, make the case faster and are easier to keep up to date.

What we're proposing

A short table setting out what each category's fee is based on, in place of "please get in touch".

Why

Cost is usually the first question a prospective member has, and the FAQ answers it by asking them to email. The Full membership page mentions a subscription and a levy without saying what either is based on. Explaining the basis, with or without figures, removes a reason not to get in touch and saves the membership team a call that only exists to answer it.

How much does it cost to become a member?

For information on how much it costs to be a member of the ABPI, please get in touch via membership@abpi.org.uk to arrange a phone call or face-to-face meeting with our membership services team. We will be happy to explain how to calculate the membership fee for your organisation.

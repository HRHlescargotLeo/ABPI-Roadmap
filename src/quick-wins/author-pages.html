What we're proposing

Link each blog author to a page with their role and posts, and make tags open a filtered list.

Why

Blogs show the author as plain text ("by Alan Lamb") and the tags under each post aren't links. The ABPI's specialists are part of its authority. An author page, and tags that lead somewhere, give readers a reason to read the next post.

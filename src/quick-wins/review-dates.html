Quick win 25 of 34 · Accuracy and housekeeping

What we're proposing

A report in the CMS listing pages by review date and owner, with shorter cycles for time-sensitive pages.

Why

Every content page already carries a "Last reviewed" and "Next review" date, which is more than most sites manage. Stale copy still gets through: the Code page was last reviewed in September 2024 and isn't due again until 2027. A simple report of what is due, by owner, with six-month cycles for pages that carry dates or deadlines, turns the dates into a working process.

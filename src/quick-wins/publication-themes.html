What we're proposing

Merge the publication themes into a short list that matches the "Our focus" menu.

Why

The theme filter has grown one tag at a time. Many themes hold a single item, and some are near-duplicates: "ATMP" and "Advanced Therapy Medicinal Products (ATMPs)"; "Clinical research" and "Clinical trials", with seven items each. Twelve themes that match the site's own menu make the filter usable, and retagging the back catalogue is a one-off job.

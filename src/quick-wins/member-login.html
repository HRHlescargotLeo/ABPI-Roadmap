Quick win 7 of 34 · Members

What we're proposing

A "Member area" link in the header of every page, opening a sign-in for the extranet.

Why

Members are told they get "access to our extranet" when they join, but there is no login link anywhere on the site, in the header or the footer. Members presumably bookmark it or search for it. A link in the header is the smallest possible change and it signals to everyone else that there is something behind the door.

Quick win 27 of 34 · Accuracy and housekeeping

What we're proposing

Correct the copy, add a search box and popular links, and check broken links each month.

Why

The page-not-found page reads "Here are some recommended pages that may interested you", then lists the latest news. Its feedback widget has 372 responses, about twice the homepage's 187, which suggests a lot of people end up there. A search box and the pages people most often look for would help them, and a monthly broken-link report would mean fewer arrive.

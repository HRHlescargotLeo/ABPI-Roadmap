Working on behalf of member companies of all sizes. There is an opportunity for our member companies to be engaged with a wide range of industry activities. Our Smaller Companies Committee is dedicated to supporting and addressing the specific issues and needs impacting this particular defined group of companies. Three members of the Smaller Companies Committee also sit on the ABPI Board in order that this sector of industry is also reflected in wider ABPI considerations and approaches.

What we're proposing

Turn the members list into a directory people can search by name, category and therapy area, with each company's logo, UK sites and a link to its own site. Give members an obvious way in to the member area from here and from every page.

Why

Today the list is company names in alphabetical groups, with a "Back To Top" link after each letter and nothing else. It is also the best evidence the ABPI has of its reach, so it is worth more than that to a company deciding whether to join. Separately, members are told they get access to an extranet, but there is no login link anywhere on the site. Every one of the five associations we compared has one on its homepage.

Member names are the ABPI's real full members. Therapy areas, UK sites and all affiliates are sample data.

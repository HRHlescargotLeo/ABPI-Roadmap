What we're proposing

One membership page that sets the three categories side by side, a checker that tells a company which one it fits in three questions, a plain account of how fees are worked out, and an online enquiry that replaces the application form.

Why

At the moment the FAQ asks companies to download the application form, which turns out to be a ZIP file dated 2022, answer every question, sign it and post it. Fees are "get in touch", and the whole process takes four to six weeks. None of that is unusual for a trade association, but it does mean the first thing a prospective member does is the most laborious part. An online enquiry costs the membership team nothing extra and gives them the details they need before the first call.

Figures in [brackets], fee bases and the contact's details are placeholders for the ABPI to supply. Benefit wording is drafted from the current membership pages and needs checking.

What we're proposing

A way to sign up for email by topic, with press releases under embargo for journalists. Alongside it, a media centre that separates press releases, statements and blogs, and event listings that say plainly whether there are places left.

Why

We couldn't find a newsletter or email alert sign-up anywhere on the site: not on the homepage, the news pages or in the footer. The ABPI publishes something most weeks, so the people who would most like to hear about it, officials, journalists and member staff, have to remember to come and look. Three of the five associations we compared offer email sign-up from the homepage.

Nothing is sent from this prototype. Topics follow the proposed publication themes and can be changed.

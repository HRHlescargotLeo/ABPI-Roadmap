What we're proposing

One layout for every policy topic: our position in a few lines, the key figures, the latest publications, a featured report with its findings and a suggested citation, related news and events, and the people to talk to. Shown here with clinical research.

Why

"Content hubs" currently has three entries, and the policy pages under "Our focus" each do things differently: some are a long page of text, some mix a graphic, positions and links. The material is all there. A consistent hub means a civil servant or a journalist who has used one topic page knows how to use the rest, and each page is fed from the publications library and news rather than kept up by hand.

Position points and figures come from the ABPI's clinical trials report and Facts and figures page. Names, roles and anything in [brackets] are placeholders.

Clinical research

Industry clinical trials bring new treatments to NHS patients early, income to the NHS and investment to the UK. We want the UK to be a first choice for them again.

Our position in brief

  • Less than a decade ago the UK was the leading European destination for industry clinical trials. Its recovery since the pandemic has been slower than other countries'.
  • Industry trials started in the UK rose to 578 in 2024, from 426 in 2023, but remain below the 667 of 2017.
  • The government's commitment to cut set-up times in England from 250 days to 150 needs to be delivered and tracked in the open.

What we're proposing

A public member area page that does two jobs. For members, it is the front door: sign in, then the latest briefings, their groups and member events. For everyone else, it shows what is behind the door, with locked previews of real briefings and a list of the groups members can join.

Why

The membership pages list weekly updates, the Chief Executive's update and UK nations updates as benefits, but a prospective member never sees one. Showing the titles and a line of each, locked, is the cheapest way to make the case for joining. The groups list does the same for influence: full members can apply to join board sponsored groups and project teams, but the groups themselves aren't listed anywhere.

Sign-in is simulated: any email and password work. Briefing titles, group names and chairs are placeholders.
